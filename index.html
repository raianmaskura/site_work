Why Maskura

What holds six businesses together.

Scale alone doesn't build trust. These are the things a partner, supplier or employee can rely on across every Maskura company.

Diversified, not scattered

Chemicals, logistics, technology, e-commerce, digital marketing and trading share infrastructure, standards and leadership — so scale in one business strengthens the others.

Built across three markets

Operating in Bangladesh, the United Kingdom and China gives Maskura a direct line between sourcing, logistics and end markets.

Technology-led by design

Our stated vision — accelerating commerce with advanced technology — is a working mandate for every company in the group, not a slogan on the website.

Values that don't flex by market

Honesty, hard work and creativity are the terms Maskura is willing to be judged on, in every country it operates in.

Build your career across a global group.

Working at Maskura means moving between industries, not staying boxed into one — from chemicals trading to e-commerce to technology, under one set of values.
