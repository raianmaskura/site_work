About Maskura

A diversified group built around one practical idea.

Since 2011, Maskura has developed businesses that solve commercial problems, enable faster movement and connect Bangladesh with international markets.

Our Story

Built by connecting capabilities, not collecting companies.

Maskura began with trade and sourcing. As customer and operating needs evolved, the group expanded into chemicals, logistics, e-commerce, technology and digital marketing.

Every new business was created around a real bottleneck. Today each company retains specialist focus while sharing a common philosophy, international network and operating standard.

Our Journey

Progress shaped by market needs.
